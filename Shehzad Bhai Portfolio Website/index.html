About Simon

Simon Sinek is an unshakable optimist who believes in a bright future and our ability to build it together. He is the author of multiple best-selling books including "Start with Why," "Leaders Eat Last," and "The Infinite Game."

His TED Talk on "How Great Leaders Inspire Action" is one of the most-watched TED Talks of all time, with over 50 million views.

Simon's WHY is to inspire people to do what inspires them so that, together, each of us can change our world for the better.
